Research

Research program examines how individuals and communities seek, interpret, and act on information in complex digital environments. Recent projects include AI literacy in schools, cross platform analyses of health misinformation, and studies of how metrics influence scholarly communication.

  • Human centered AI and literacy. Co design of AI literacy curricula with school librarians and educators.
  • Health misinformation. Mixed methods analyses of COVID 19 and HIV content across YouTube, TikTok, and other platforms.
  • Science of science. Bibliometrics and altmetrics to understand research impact, equity, and collaboration networks.

Teaching

Teaching portfolio covers information science, data visualization, research methods, and AI literacy. Courses are designed to connect theory and practice and to prepare students for data informed and AI enabled workplaces.

  • Information visualization and storytelling with data.
  • Human centered AI and information systems.
  • Research methods in information science.

Selected publications

Publications appear in venues such as JAMA Network Open, Information Processing and Management, PLOS ONE, and Scientometrics. A full list is available on Google Scholar.

  • Studies of how health information and misinformation spread on social media platforms.
  • Analyses of the opportunities and risks of generative AI for teaching and research.
  • Work on alternative metrics, research evaluation, and the responsible use of indicators.
